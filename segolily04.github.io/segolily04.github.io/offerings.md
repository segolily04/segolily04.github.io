# Offerings

Every engagement is scoped as a clear project with a defined outcome, so you know what you are getting before we start.

## 1. AI governance for contracts and commercial operations

For energy and infrastructure companies that want to use AI without creating compliance headaches.

- A review of where AI touches your contracting and commercial work
- Practical guardrails and approval steps your team will actually follow
- Plain-language documentation for leadership and regulators

## 2. Automation and AI agent builds

For teams buried in repeat tasks like invoices, reconciliation, and reporting.

- A working prototype built around your tools (QuickBooks, Gmail, Google Workspace)
- Built-in checks so a human stays in charge of the final call
- A handoff so you can run and maintain it

## 3. Human-first AI consulting

For individuals and small businesses who want to thrive with AI in a fast, extractive world, without burning out or losing their voice.

- Right-sized tools and workflows for how you really work
- Coaching that makes space for your pace and your values
